Jaipur - The Pink City

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan, India. It is famous for its pink-coloured buildings, grand forts and colourful bazaars. (Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II)

About the Place

Jaipur blends royal history with a lively modern city. Its palaces, markets and local food attract visitors from all over the world.



Location        : Rajasthan, India
Best Time       : October to March
Famous For      : Forts, palaces, handicrafts

Attractions

  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar

Activities

  1. Take an elephant-free guided tour of Amber Fort
  2. Shop for jewellery and textiles at Johari Bazaar
  3. Taste Rajasthani food like dal baati churma
